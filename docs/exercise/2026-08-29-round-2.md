# Round 2 Exercise Report, 2026-08-29

## What round 2 is

The post-fix-round re-exercise of the live P3 system. Round 1 (`2026-08-28-p3-exercise.md`) filed 43 findings and six candidate workstreams; the fix round shipped against them. This report re-runs the same live stack to answer three questions: which round-1 findings actually closed, what the fixes traded away, and what the supersession precision number is now that the propose-only pin has a measurement behind it.

Not the test suite. Every claim below comes from a tool call, a graph read, a SQLite query, a service log line, a direct probe of Ollama, or a code read against current `main`. Load-bearing code citations in this report were re-verified during synthesis, not carried on trust from the angle reports.

Window: 2026-08-29, roughly 12:47Z to 13:35Z by the service log, against the live dev instance at `127.0.0.1:8765`. Three angles ran concurrently against one shared instance with overlapping windows, so each angle's latency numbers describe the instance under the load the others were also applying. ## Verdict

The fix round bought honesty and did not buy recall. Round 1 called this a good recorder and an unreliable rememberer; round 2 measures a good recorder that now admits when it does not know, and still cannot reliably tell you what it does know. The honesty half is real and verified three ways: round 1's own absent-topic fixtures now return genuinely empty packs (0 items and 23 tokens against 4,232 episodes, where the same literal query filled 1,157 to 1,199 tokens in round 1), packs run at a third of the token budget instead of saturating it (token p50 402 against a 1,200 cap, where 16 of 17 round-1 packs landed between 1,111 and 1,194), the supersession judge created zero closures across 63 stage runs and 156 contradiction judgments against round 1's three wrong auto-applies, and the operator surface went from nothing to `status`, `queue`, `doctor` and `/health` reporting depth, oldest-unclaimed age, exhausted jobs, lag, reinforcement drops and open proposal counts. Interactive freshness is the round's clearest win: ack to enriched ran 8.8s to 28.5s on a rate-capped mixed load and 50s to 576s under eight concurrent sessions, against round 1's 174s to about 2,900s. Storage is bounded where round 1 found it unbounded: `reinforcement_queue` holds exactly 50,000 rows with a live drop counter instead of an unbounded 68,519-to-79,593 trajectory, and the Neo4j volume shrank to 1.502GB from 1.729GB while the graph itself grew 13%. The recall half regressed in the way the floor's design invited. Ten of twenty-five on-topic questions came back with no answer in them, every one a "what did we decide" or "how did we fix", while the nodes that answer them measure 0.60 to 0.80 cosine against those very queries, at or above the 0.60 floor they are supposed to clear. Two mechanisms, both measured: ten seeds are merged across every leg against a 6,600-node memory population with no per-cue reservation, so retrieval is effectively lexical for anything the seeds miss (93 of 191 packed items came from bm25, 14 of them exact literal matches), and spreading activation now contributes zero items to every pack because the admission gate refuses an activation arrival without ever measuring it, dropping 989 of 1,467 candidates unmeasured. Supersession precision measured 0.400 over 15 scoreable proposals, with all 16 proposals at or above the shipped 0.85 auto gate, so the propose-only pin is correct and the confidence threshold behind it separates nothing. And the correction path still does not close: applying a correct proposal at claim level left recall answering with the old owner at rank 1, stamped `current`, because the stale claim also lives in a frozen entity gloss that supersession puts out of scope by construction. As exercised today this is a system that will tell you honestly when it knows nothing, and will still not reliably tell you what it knows.

### Blockers

#### R2-1. A genuinely on-topic recall returns a pack with no answer in it
**Area:** recall. **Angle:** usage. **Descends from:** EX-20 (the seed budget), EX-19, EX-1.

**Symptom.** An on-topic recall returns a confident, on-topic-looking pack that omits the thing it was asked for, while the answering nodes measure 0.60 to 0.80 cosine against the query. The pack carries no honesty note, so the caller cannot tell.

**Repro.** Store an incident session (spike, root cause, mitigation, prevention decision), let every episode enrich, then in a fresh session recall "how did we fix the checkout latency" bare, with a generic summary, and with a specific summary; then "what fixed the checkout latency spike", "what did we do to bring checkout p95 back down", and the same query at `budget.max_tokens` 4000.

**Evidence.** Six natural phrasings returned 6 to 13 items each and not one stated the fix. Graph truth, all `valid_until` null and all `EXTRACTED_FROM` episode `6463d75d`: Insight `2fec0c56` "Concurrent index creation can effectively reduce checkout latency without compromising data integrity."; Event `28a9b6cc` "The orders.customer_id index was created concurrently at 15:05 UTC."; Decision `52b6ba21` "Recreate the missing index on orders.customer_id to resolve the sequential scan performance issue." Measured against "how did we fix the checkout latency" through the product's own lowercasing path on host `nomic-embed-text`, against the vectors stored on the nodes: `2fec0c56` content 0.7202 / context 0.8039; `28a9b6cc` content 0.6003 / context 0.8039; `52b6ba21` content 0.6094 / context 0.6061. The reported `vector_floor` is 0.60, so all three clear it, and all three are absent from every one of the six packs. Admitted instead, at lower measurement: an unrelated round-1 goal "Cut checkout p99 latency from 2.4 seconds to under 800 milliseconds by the end of Q3." at confidence 0.76, and "p99 latency (concept)" at 0.72. The 4000-token run rules out the packer. Adding the node's literal words ("concurrent index creation checkout latency") brings the same Insight to rank 1 at confidence 0.88 via bm25, so the node is reachable and the vector path is what fails. Same shape on a second topic: Decision `1690395c` "Rejecting the Redis mutex approach in favor of PostgreSQL's row-level lock." reaches rank 1 on one phrasing (content 0.6699) and is absent from two others measuring 0.5618/0.6283 and 0.6233/0.6624. Across 25 scored recalls, 10 on-topic questions returned no answer and 2 more returned only the topic without the claim.

**Suspected cause.** Not the admission floor: every missed item measured at or above 0.60, so it was never a candidate rather than judged and dropped. The candidate set is the constraint. `packages/core/src/infrastructure/config/defaults.ts:132` sets `seedLimit: 10`, and `packages/core/src/recall/application/seeds.ts:493` merges every leg's contributions through `mergeSeeds(contributions, deps.config.contextResonance.seedLimit)`, so ten seeds cover a 6,600-node memory population with no per-cue-class or per-leg reservation. `considered` ran 22 to 80 (p50 61). Retrieval is therefore effectively lexical for anything the ten seeds miss: 93 of 191 packed items came from bm25 and 14 were exact literal matches. Verified in code during synthesis: `seeds.ts:450` names `seedLimit` as "the seed budget and the only place the candidate set is" bounded.

**Fix shape.** Scale the seed budget with substrate size and reserve slots per cue class and per leg, so the vector leg cannot be crowded out by lexical and recency contributions. Measure recall on a held-out set of stored-claim and natural-question pairs rather than on absent-topic probes alone: the gate's battery 2 checks that on-topic packs are non-empty, which this failure passes while returning the wrong contents.

**Related.** R2-8 (the ranking half of the same user-visible failure), R2-9 (the vector that would have found these).

#### R2-2. Spreading activation contributes zero items to every pack
**Area:** recall. **Angle:** usage. **Descends from:** EX-1 (the fix that caused this), EX-21, EX-38.

**Symptom.** Any node reached by traversal alone is refused by the admission gate without ever being measured against the query, even though the query embedding is already computed and scoring the node is one dot product. The traversal the graph exists for now returns nothing.

**Repro.** Serve any recall, read `metadata.admission.dropped_unmeasured`, then census `rationale.method` across the returned items.

**Evidence.** Across 25 recalls: bm25 93, vector 77, entity_resolution 20, recency 1, activation 0, graph_traversal 0, resonance 0. Independently, over the 15 packs held in `last_pack` since 10:00Z from mixed clients: entity_resolution 13, vector 56, bm25 43, recency 1, activation 0. `dropped_unmeasured` totalled 989 of 1,467 candidates considered in the 25-recall set (67.4%) and 611 of 882 in the `last_pack` set (69.3%); p50 was 44 unmeasured per recall against a p50 of 9 admitted. Round 1 measured activation producing 211 of 1,827 packed items (11.6%), so the leg went from contributing to contributing nothing.

**Suspected cause.** The fix for EX-1 (traversed items admitted with no floor of their own) was implemented as refusal rather than measurement. Verified in code: `packages/core/src/recall/domain/admission.ts:70` defines `COSINE_METHODS` as exactly `vector`, `entity_resolution`, `resonance`, with the comment "Recency and activation are absent because neither measures how well a node answers the query", and `droppedUnmeasured` is documented as reached by spreading activation alone with no method measuring it against the query. Activation reaches a node and hands it to the gate carrying an activation score, which is not a cosine, so the gate drops it. Nothing computes the node's `content_vec` against the already-embedded query before dropping it.

**Synthesis note.** The deltas angle independently measured that activation truncation improved sharply, `node_budget` termination falling from 60.2% to 7.9% and the frontier no longer pinned at 50. Those two results together mean the spread now runs further and cleaner and produces nothing, so it is currently pure cost inside the recall path.

**Fix shape.** Measure activation arrivals instead of discarding them: score the reached node's `content_vec` against the query embedding and put it through the same floor every seed faces. That keeps the honesty the floor bought and restores the traversal. Until then, report in `/health` or `doctor` that activation is admitting nothing, because 69% of candidates dropped unmeasured reads as a working leg.

#### R2-3. Supersession precision is 0.400, and confidence separates nothing
**Area:** reflection-pipeline. **Angle:** precision. **Descends from:** EX-7.

**Symptom.** Measured precision is 0.400, not the 0.9 the propose-only pin requires before auto-apply returns. Every proposal the run produced scores at or above the shipped `supersedeAutoConfidence` of 0.85, so the confidence gate separates nothing.

**Repro.** 24 cases (8 true contradictions, 8 false baits, 8 hard), ground truth pre-committed before the run. Wave A stores the first fact of each case into a fresh MCP session; wait for full enrichment; wave B stores the second fact; wait again. Then read `supersession_proposals` and score each proposal against the pre-committed label.

**Evidence.** Baseline 2026-08-29T12:51:00Z. Stage ledger over the window: 63 supersession runs, 11 skipped for no fact-bearing nodes, 156 contradiction judgments, 0 superseded, 16 proposed, 16 by shared subject. 15 of the 16 trace to the battery's own 48 episodes (the 16th is the concurrent usage angle's episode `6463d75d`, excluded as unscoreable). Scored: TP 6, FN 6, FP 9. Precision 6/15 = 0.400, recall 6/12 = 0.500, precision restricted to the case's own planted pair 6/9 = 0.667. Confidence over all 16: 15 at exactly 1.0 and 1 at 0.95, minimum 0.95, and 16 of 16 at or above 0.85. Had `AION_SUPERSEDE_MODE=auto` been set, all 15 scoreable proposals would have become irreversible `supersede()` closures and 9 of them would be wrong. Verified in code during synthesis: `packages/core/src/reflection/application/stages/supersession.ts:45` sets `DEFAULT_SUPERSEDE_AUTO_CONFIDENCE = 0.85`, unreachable as a filter against that distribution. The wire lane independently confirms the propose-only behavior holds from the protocol boundary: a planted contradiction flowed detection to proposal to CLI apply with zero auto-closures throughout.

**Suspected cause.** Two independent drivers, filed separately: candidate generation admits a shared generic noun as a shared subject (R2-4, 6 of the 9 wrong proposals), and the judge has no discrimination for scope-widening or for attribution-stripped opinions (R2-12, R2-13, 3 of 9). The confidence field carries no signal at all, which is EX-7's observation reproduced with a larger sample.

**Fix shape.** Keep `propose` pinned. Do not re-enable auto on a confidence threshold: the model does not produce a usable confidence distribution, so any threshold below 0.95 admits everything and any threshold above it admits nothing. If auto is wanted later, gate it on a structural precondition the pipeline can check without the model (the same named entity is the grammatical subject on both sides, and the two claims assign different values to the same attribute), then measure precision again on that gate alone. The full breakdown is in its own section below.

#### R2-4. The subject-identity leg accepts a shared generic noun as proof of a shared subject
**Area:** reflection-pipeline. **Angle:** precision. **Descends from:** EX-6.

**Symptom.** Any extracted entity name that is a substring of the new claim counts as subject identity. Generic technical nouns are extracted as entities, so "kafka", "orders table", "timeout", "sync" and "push" all certify two unrelated claims as being about the same thing. This produced 6 of the 9 false proposals, every one against an unrelated node in the pre-existing substrate, and it consumed the candidate slots the real pair needed.

**Repro.** Store any episode whose claim names a widely-used technology, for example a reflection whose observation is "We decided to use Kafka for Bramble notification fanout." Wait for enrichment, then read `supersession_proposals`.

**Evidence.** Quoted from the created rows. Case T2, confidence 1.0, rationale "the choice of messaging system for notification fanout": OLD Decision "Pub/Sub was chosen over Kafka due to its managed status and existing usage" superseded by NEW "We decided to use Kafka for Bramble notification fanout." Two different systems, both still true. Their cosine is 0.6065, below the 0.75 KNN threshold, so this candidate arrived purely through the shared-subject leg. Case T6, confidence 1.0, rationale "orders table": OLD Decision "We will not shard the orders table." superseded by NEW "The team decided against sharding the Wexford orders table." The two statements agree. Read-only replay of `SUBJECT_IDENTITY_CANDIDATES` per wave-B subject shows the matched name: T2 returns three rows with `shared='kafka'` (0.689, 0.663, 0.648) beside the one correct row with `shared='bramble'` (0.785); T1 returns three rows with `shared='timeout'` including "Cue call timeout refers to a situation where the system fails to respond" and "The Peregrine search API write timeout is set to 45 seconds"; H8 returns `shared='sync'` and `shared='push'` rows about unrelated subsystems. In every case the generic-noun rows crowd out or sit beside the real pair inside a 3-candidate limit.

**Suspected cause.** Confirmed in code. `packages/core/src/infrastructure/graph/supersession-queries.ts:166-192`, `SUBJECT_IDENTITY_CANDIDATES`, qualifies a candidate when `$subjectTextNorm CONTAINS e.name_norm` and `n.text_norm CONTAINS name`, with the only guard being `MIN_SUBJECT_NAME_LENGTH = 3` at line 39. Verified during synthesis, including the constant's own comment, which reasons only about one- and two-character surface forms. A bare substring test over a lowercased fold cannot separate "the subject of this claim" from "a technology this claim mentions". The second disjunct is looser: a candidate also qualifies by hanging off any episode that mentions the entity, which pulls in whole unrelated sessions.

**Fix shape.** Require the shared entity to be the subject of both claims, not merely present in both: prefer an entity that heads the claim, or require the two claims to agree on the same entity plus the same attribute. Failing that, weight entity names by substrate-wide mention frequency and refuse a name appearing across more than a small fraction of episodes. "kafka" and "orders table" are corpus vocabulary, "bramble" and "wisteria" are subjects, and the frequency separates them cleanly. Drop the episode-mention disjunct or restrict it to the same session.

#### R2-5. Applying a correct proposal does not change what recall answers
**Area:** recall, quality. **Angle:** precision. **Descends from:** EX-5, EX-18.

**Symptom.** The judged fact is closed and correctly marked, and the entity gloss carrying the same stale claim stays open, keeps rank 1, and is stamped `currency: current`. The review path's purpose, that a stored correction changes what recall answers, is unmet in the default apply mode.

**Repro.** Store "Dmitri Volkov owns the Quillon ingest pipeline.", let it enrich, store "Dmitri Volkov no longer owns the Quillon ingest pipeline. Anneke Vos owns the Quillon ingest pipeline now.", let it enrich, run `proposals apply <id>` on the resulting proposal, then re-run `recall({query:'who owns the Quillon ingest pipeline'})`.

**Evidence.** Pre-apply pack, all items marked current: rank 1 "Quillon ingest pipeline (project): The pipeline that Dmitri Volkov owns and is responsible for." (bm25 1.00), rank 3 "Quillon ingest pipeline is owned by Dmitri Volkov." (vector 0.88), rank 4 "Anneke Vos (person): Current owner of the Quillon ingest pipeline." (vector 0.84). Proposal `138d2975` applied successfully: "closed 1 node(s), superseded by 6d5daf99", with the Concept closed at 2026-08-29T13:19:38.651Z and a SUPERSEDES edge carrying provenance `['supersession_proposal_applied']`. Post-apply pack: rank 1 is still "Quillon ingest pipeline (project): The pipeline that Dmitri Volkov owns and is responsible for.", marked current; rank 4 and rank 6 carry the same stale ownership. A direct graph read confirms all three Entity nodes have `valid_until IS NULL`. The correct answer sits at rank 3 and the correction at rank 9. Contrast case T7, applied with `--episode`: the stale Event left the pack entirely and rank 3 became the corrected claim, a clean flip. The wire lane reached the same conclusion independently from the CLI side (W2-4) and rated it working-as-designed ergonomics; this report rates it a blocker because it is the load-bearing failure of the whole correction path, and the two ratings are the same observation with different scope.

**Suspected cause.** By construction. The supersession stage header (`stages/supersession.ts:26-28`) puts entities out of scope because extraction merges them on `(name_norm, type)` and there is no new node to supersede them with, and `applySupersessionProposal` without `episode` closes exactly `proposal.oldId` (`proposals.ts:113-121`). Nothing re-derives an entity's frozen `text` from its current facts, which is EX-18 still standing.

**Fix shape.** Stop serving a frozen entity description as a fact. Either re-derive the gloss from the entity's currently-open linked facts at enrichment time, or drop entity `text` from the facts bucket and render an entity from its current claims at pack time. Until then the node-level apply should warn that entity glosses still carry the old value, rather than printing only the `--episode` hint.

**Related.** R2-8 (entity glosses take 48% of fact slots), R2-14 (the mode that does flip the answer, and what it costs).

#### R2-6. Decision nodes store a faithful rationale and the pack never renders it
**Area:** recall, quality. **Angle:** usage. **Descends from:** EX-19.

**Symptom.** "Why did we choose Y" is answerable from the graph and unanswerable from the pack, unless the reason happens to be folded into the node's own `text`.

**Repro.** `MATCH (n:Decision) RETURN count(*), count(n.rationale)`, then recall "why did we choose SELECT FOR UPDATE over a redis mutex" and read the rendered rank-1 item.

**Evidence.** 78 of 107 Decision nodes carry a rationale, and they read cleanly: `91b684ce` carries "2.x has no ledger table, so the refund race cannot occur there, and a backport would require a different fix for a bug that does not exist."; `1690395c` carries "PostgreSQL already owns the ledger row, ensuring the lock is held where the data is, preventing potential race conditions." The rendered pack for that query put `1690395c` at rank 1 as "Rejecting the Redis mutex approach in favor of PostgreSQL's row-level lock. [1690395c...] | bm25 | confidence 0.67" with no reason attached. The pack carried a reason only because a separate Insight node happened to rank 2. `packages/core/src/recall/domain/pack.ts:172` builds each rendered line from `[id]`, `rationale.method` and the measurement, where `item.rationale` is the retrieval rationale (method, score, path), a name collision with the node property.

**Suspected cause.** `seed-queries.ts` projects id, text and vectors; the node's `rationale` property is never read into a candidate, so `renderItem` has nothing to print.

**Fix shape.** Select `rationale` alongside `text` for fact-bearing nodes and render it as a second line under the claim. This is the cheapest large quality win available: the extraction is already correct and already stored.

#### R2-7. Narratives moved from confabulation to vacuity, because they race enrichment
**Area:** reflection-pipeline. **Angle:** usage. **Descends from:** EX-8, EX-32.

**Symptom.** Round 1's confabulation is gone and the replacement says nothing. Narratives now cite real episodes and restate their summaries and timestamps, naming none of the session's decisions or findings. Where one ventures a substantive claim it can be wrong. They are written at transport close, which races enrichment, so the extracted nodes the grounding rule would draw on do not exist yet, and there is no regeneration afterwards.

**Repro.** Run a 4-reflection working session, close the transport, then read the Narrative node against the session's episodes and against the cognitive nodes that arrive later.

**Evidence.** Session `17e19d9f` (4 episodes: a refund race, a Redis-mutex rejection, a timezone test bug, a no-backport decision) produced "The user reviewed PR 412 on the reconciliation service in three consecutive episodes before finishing the review." with `coverage 1`, `coverage_count 4`, `grounding 'grounded-1'`, 4 citations, `version 1`. Grounded, and wrong: the session held four episodes. Session `36028c67` (spike, root cause, mitigation, prevention decision) produced "investigating a checkout latency spike", verbatim the `summary` string sent on its first episode, with 1 citation against `coverage_count 4`. Session `4b9c04a7` (one observation about a ledger export rounding to two decimals and losing eleven cents on a thousand-line invoice) produced "The ledger rounding note was recorded during the episode tagged 2026-08-29T12:55:42.521Z.", the title and the timestamp, not the fact. Timing proves the race: `17e19d9f`'s narrative `tx_from` is 12:49:26.695Z while its four episodes' orchestrator ledger keys landed at 12:49:55, 12:50:46, 12:57:31 and 12:59:01, so zero of its episodes had cognitive nodes when it was narrated; `36028c67` narrated at 12:50:22.578Z against enrichments at 12:51:24 through 12:59:21. All three are `version 1` and nothing re-narrates them. They reach packs: 5 of 23 non-empty packs opened at rank 1 with a narrative, and "investigating a checkout latency spike" was rank 1 on both "what caused the checkout latency incident" (0.88) and "how did we fix the checkout latency" (0.86).

**Suspected cause.** `packages/core/src/reflection/domain/narrative.ts` defines `NarrativeExtractedNode` and permits extracted nodes as citable sources, so the design is right, but `bootstrap.ts:279` fires `SessionNarrativeCloser.onSessionClosed` on transport close with no gate on the session's enrichment state, and `narratives.ts:294` loads `loadSessionEpisodes` whether or not anything has been extracted from them. The only regenerate path is the manual `narrative-cleanup-run.ts`.

**Fix shape.** Do not narrate a session until its episodes have applied their orchestrator ledger key, or narrate then and re-narrate when enrichment finishes; the supersession path for narratives already exists. Require at least one cited node that is not the episode's own summary, so a narrative that can only restate titles is not stored at all.

#### R2-8. Decision-intent queries still lose the Decision
**Area:** recall, ranking. **Angle:** usage. **Descends from:** EX-19, EX-5.

**Symptom.** The cue model correctly stamps `intent: decision` and the pack then fills with topic-adjacent glosses, restatements of the question, and contentless "a decision was made" nodes, while the Decision that answers it is absent. Round 1's exact wrong-memory scenario still reproduces, and one query returns a different incident's decision with nothing marking the switch.

**Repro.** Recall "what did we decide about the refund locking approach", "what did we decide about preventing this kind of migration bug", "what did we decide about the checkout incident follow up", round 1's own "what did we decide about how the remittance files get ingested?", and a control on an absent topic, "what did we decide about the sourdough starter hydration ratio".

**Evidence.** D1: 6 items, the answering Decision `1690395c` absent; rank 1 is a Context node "The reconciliation service requires reliable locking mechanisms to prevent data inconsistencies during refund operations." D3: 11 items, the answering Plan `9f7dcea2` absent; the pack is entirely a different incident's migration content ("Implement a lock timeout convention in migration templates", "A migration that passed locally deadlocked in staging"), so an agent reading it answers with the wrong fix. D4: 8 items, the follow-up Plan absent; rank 1 is "checkout latency incident (event): The event being addressed in the summary and observations.", rank 5 is "Wexford (organization)" admitted by the `recency` leg, rank 6 is "The decision was made in the context of beta episode 4." Round 1's case: Decision `2c0f6c3b` "Use a Postgres outbox table plus a polling worker for remittance ingest." is still absent at content 0.5789 / context 0.6851, while the query-restating Goal "Select an appropriate transport mechanism for remittance ingest." (0.7102) is admitted at rank 11 and the stale, wrong "Webhooks were selected as the ingestion method." (0.5590 / 0.6003) at rank 12. Round 1 flagged exactly that item as the one that makes an agent conclude the system ingests via webhooks. Control: the absent decision topic returned 3 items rather than an empty pack, including "The decision was made in the context of beta episode 4." at 0.63 and "Wexford (organization)" at 0.61. That contentless node cleared the floor on four separate decision-intent queries in this run. Across 23 non-empty packs, 9 opened with an entity gloss and 16 carried one in the top two; entity glosses were 71 of 148 fact slots (48%, against 58% in round 1).

**Suspected cause.** The decision intent reaches the cue set and nothing downstream boosts by node type. A contentless node whose text is "The decision was made in the context of X" is maximally similar to "what did we decide about X" while carrying no answer. Entity glosses stay short and lexically dense, which wins bm25 and the budget fit at once; `entityGlossCap` in `pack.ts` limits how many reach the pack but not where they rank.

**Fix shape.** Use `CueIntent.decision` for what it was added for: rank Decision and Insight above Goal, Plan, Context and Entity when it is set. Refuse to mint or to admit nodes whose text names a decision without stating one. Never let the `recency` leg place an item in a decision-intent pack.

#### R2-9. `context_vec` is recomputed on every enrichment and no recall leg reads it
**Area:** recall, substrate. **Angle:** usage. **Descends from:** EX-38.

**Symptom.** The vector that would have surfaced the answers the packs are missing is written, indexed, and never read.

**Repro.** `SHOW INDEXES` confirms `context_vec_idx` exists; grep every recall query for it.

**Evidence.** Verified during synthesis: `context_vec_idx` appears in `migrations.ts:41` (creation), `introspection.ts:6` (the doctor check), two comments in `labels.ts` and `migrations.ts`, and two test files. No recall path reads it. Every seed leg uses `CONTENT_VECTOR_INDEX = 'content_vec_idx'` (`seed-queries.ts:25,197`; `association-queries.ts:153`). **Lineage rendering is new and works.** A superseded item comes back in the pack marked `[superseded]` with "superseded by `<id>` at `<timestamp>`" in the rendered block, which round 1 had nothing of. `floor-calibration` is a real self-report rather than a number: "floor 0.600 (corroboration 0.550), unrelated n=28 p50 0.410 p95 0.532, related n=10 min 0.451 p05 0.513 p50 0.773, 20% of genuine matches under the floor and carried by corroboration". `doctor` now warns on `redaction-residue` (13 of 5,000 nodes still secret-shaped, naming ids), so round 1's eight-of-eight-green-while-hours-behind is gone.

**Activation truncation improved.** `node_budget` termination fell from 60.2% (at 4,127 episodes) to 7.9% (at 4,315 episodes) with the caps byte-identical in `defaults.ts`, and `activated` is no longer pinned at exactly 50 on every recall. Read alongside R2-2, this means the spread now runs further and cleaner and contributes nothing.

## The supersession precision number

This is the auto-mode decision input. It is one designed battery, one instance, one window; it is the only measurement that exists.

**Headline: precision 0.400, recall 0.500. Keep `propose` pinned.**

Method: 24 cases, ground truth pre-committed before the run. Wave A stores the first fact of each case into a fresh MCP session; wait for full enrichment; wave B stores the second fact; wait again. Detection runs during wave B enrichment. Every proposal is then scored against its pre-committed label. 48 episodes, 28 sessions, 4 cases deliberately cross-session, rate-capped throughout.

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Scoreable proposals | 15 (of 16; one is a concurrent angle's episode, excluded) |
| True positives | 6 (T2, T6, T7, T8, H1, H6) |
| False positives | 9 (3 against the case's own planted pair, 6 collateral against unrelated substrate) |
| False negatives | 6 (T1, T3, T4, T5, H2, H8) |
| True negatives | 10 |
| **Precision** | **0.400** |
| Precision, designed pairs only | 0.667 |
| **Recall** | **0.500** |
| Stage-created closures | 0 |

Per class:

| Case class | n | Result |
|---|---|---|
| True contradictions | 8 | 4 of 8 caught |
| False baits | 8 | 7 of 8 correctly refused |
| Hard cases, true side | 4 | 2 of 4 caught |
| Hard cases, false side | 4 | 3 of 4 correctly refused |

**The confidence distribution is the decisive finding, not the ratio.** Over all 16 proposals: 15 at exactly 1.0, 1 at 0.95. Minimum 0.95. All 16 sit at or above the shipped `DEFAULT_SUPERSEDE_AUTO_CONFIDENCE = 0.85` (`stages/supersession.ts:45`, verified against current main). There is no threshold that separates the 6 correct proposals from the 9 wrong ones, because the model emits the same number for both. Any threshold below 0.95 admits everything; anything above it admits nothing. Had auto mode been enabled, all 15 scoreable proposals would have become irreversible closures and 9 of them would have closed a claim that is still true.

**Where the errors live, which is not where the propose-only pin implies.**

| Error | n | Root cause |
|---|---|---|
| False negative | 3 | First fact landed on a label the scan does not read (Plan, Context, or no cognitive node at all) |
| False negative | 2 | Discriminating value paraphrased out of the claim; judge correctly refused a pair that no longer contradicts |
| False negative | 1 | Subject name paraphrased out; never became a candidate |
| False positive | 6 | Subject-identity leg accepted a shared generic noun ("kafka", "orders table", "timeout", "sync") |
| False positive | 3 | Judge: scope widening judged as contradiction (2), attribution-stripped opinion judged as correction (1) |

Zero of the six misses are the judge reasoning badly about a well-formed pair. Six of the nine false positives never reach the judge's discretion at all; they are candidate-generation errors that the judge then rationalizes. See R2-4, R2-11, R2-12, R2-13.

**Stage cost.** 63 runs, 11 skipped for no fact-bearing nodes, 156 contradiction judgments, 198,039ms total, 14.1% of pipeline wall time, p50 3,007ms, p95 9,032ms, max 10,415ms. All 16 proposals came from the subject-identity leg and none from the KNN leg (R2-20).

**Recommendation.** Keep `propose` pinned. Do not re-enable auto on a confidence threshold at any value. If auto is wanted later, gate it on a structural precondition the pipeline can check without the model, specifically that the same named entity is the grammatical subject on both sides and that the two claims assign different values to the same attribute, then re-measure precision on that gate alone. Treat the auto-mode question as blocked on extraction fidelity (R2-11) and entity-gloss staleness (R2-5) rather than on the judge. Precision below 0.9 keeps a human in the loop, which is survivable; an applied correction that leaves recall answering the old value at rank 1 marked `current` means the human in the loop cannot fix it either.
